# Course Hero research and adaptation

Research date: 14 September 2026. Scope: public pages and official support documentation. No paid subscription, tutor account, or authenticated student session was used. Publicly documented functionality can be described; private implementation, actual answer quality, personalized flows, and checkout outcomes cannot be verified here. This is a broad public-feature inventory, not a guarantee of exhaustive account-level coverage.

## 1. How the product works

Course Hero organizes study resources around schools and courses. Its homepage provides resource search, institution/course discovery, AI help, expert help, and links to other study tools. Public navigation includes textbook solutions, literature guides, writing tools, flashcards through Cram, and quizzes. Mobile applications and a browser extension are also linked. These are visible product surfaces; availability and entitlements can vary. [Course Hero homepage](https://www.coursehero.com/)

The commercial loop is discovery, preview, access, and continued study. Basic accounts can browse; paid membership provides additional access. The official membership FAQ describes 30 monthly document unlocks and plan-dependent tutor allowances. Its historical library-size claim differs from the homepage, so neither count should be used as a current audited figure. [Membership comparison](https://support.coursehero.com/hc/en-us/articles/200350884-What-s-the-difference-between-a-Premier-Membership-and-a-Basic-free-Membership)

The plans page lists Premier and Premier Plus, course resources, AI, expert support, textbook explanations, literature guides, and personalized dashboard recommendations. Dollar prices were not reliably exposed in the retrieved page; this report does not assert a current price or universal package allowance. [Plans](https://www.coursehero.com/plans/)

## 3. Observed model versus inferred mechanics

The following journey synthesizes the public material above; screen order and exact account behavior were not tested:

1. Find a relevant resource through search or a course page.
2. Inspect available information and preview content.
3. Sign in and obtain sufficient access through membership or eligible contributions.
4. View the available full resource and continue studying.
5. Use explanations, questions, and course organization to support follow-up learning.

Contribution processing and rejection are explicitly documented: rejected duplicates or irrelevant documents do not earn credit, and processing can take up to 24 hours. That does not reveal whether every upload is manually reviewed. [Upload processing FAQ](https://support.coursehero.com/hc/en-us/articles/200350704-I-uploaded-the-required-number-of-documents-why-don-t-I-have-Unlocks)

The likely growth mechanism is that more useful contributions improve discovery and recurring study value. This is a product inference, not access to Course Hero's conversion metrics, revenue, ranking algorithms, or moderation infrastructure.

## 4. What to adapt and what to change

Adopt course-aligned discovery, clear access states, organized personal study, and contribution quality controls. Build a distinct brand and original interface. Source original or licensed materials; this project should not depend on copying Course Hero's resource inventory.

Differentiate with chapter-to-learning-outcome mapping, explicit syllabus versions, teacher review dates, Nepali explanations, local payments, low-bandwidth reading, and exam recovery after connection loss. Mock exams should lead directly to relevant revision material.

Avoid a complicated economy at launch. A useful free library plus a paid study pass is easier to explain than mixing document unlocks, AI credits, tutor credits, and subscriptions. Add separate quotas only when the service costs justify them.

## 5. Preboard Nepal discovery gap

Both HTTP and HTTPS homepage retrieval failed in this research environment. This is not proof the site is offline. Site-specific searches did not reveal a dependable inventory. The local repository contains no current application to inspect.

Before migration, inventory existing URLs, traffic landing pages, content ownership, user records, authentication, payments, storage, hosting access, and redirects. Export and validate a backup. Determine whether the project replaces an existing product or launches a new application on the domain. Preserve valuable URLs through documented redirects.

## 6. Evidence confidence and follow-up

High confidence: publicly documented search, course organization, membership, upload rewards, upload restrictions, and support categories. Moderate confidence: advertised AI and partner-tool availability; actual access may vary. Unverified: current exact prices, dashboard visuals, result ranking, mobile interactions, tutor response times, internal technology, and account-specific quotas.

A later authenticated product walkthrough should test onboarding, search filters, preview length, unlock confirmation, library organization, AI citations, tutor revision handling, subscription cancellation, and download behavior. It should record observed behavior with date and account tier rather than treat marketing language as service guarantees.
